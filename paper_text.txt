================================================================================
ABSTRACT
================================================================================
Spatial transcriptomics (ST) deconvolution models (such as cell2location and RCTD) predict cell-type proportions across spots on a tissue. We propose SpotGuard, an operational, interactive conformal gatekeeping system for ST deconvolution. The SpotGuard nonconformity formulation relies on the Total Variation (TV) geometry on the simplex Delta^{K-1}. For adaptation to an atlas shift, SpotGuard uses a density-ratio weighted conformal prediction method. We implement SpotGuard within a sub-16ms interactive workstation, equipped with live miscoverage (alpha) slider controls, which automatically prunes uncalibrated spots, recovering 89.4% coverage in response to a severe atlas shift (vs. 84.6% unweighted, while Bayesian joint intervals collapse to 1.4%) and decreasing compositional Total Variation error on retained tissue by 66.2% (Area Under the Risk-Coverage curve = 0.138).

Keywords: Spatial transcriptomics, deconvolution, conformal prediction, distribution shift, oncology.


================================================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

Clinical Problem & Importance.
Spatial transcriptomics profiles gene expression across intact biopsies (Marx, 2021; Rao et al., 2021). Since sequencing measures multi-cellular mixtures (55 um spots containing 5 to 30 cells), computational deconvolution models infer cell-type proportion vectors y_hat in Delta^{K-1}. In oncology, deconvolution maps help critical decisions: measuring cytotoxic CD8+ T-cell infiltration, identifying tertiary lymphoid structures, and describing surgical margins.

Failure Mode of Current Approaches.
Deconvolution models use reference single-cell RNA sequencing atlases. If atlases and patient tissues do not match due to platform differences (such as 10x Chromium vs. Slide-seq), tissue preservation (FFPE vs. fresh frozen), or demographics, exchangeability fails. Bayesian posteriors become overconfident: physicians have no way to tell between good and bad spot calls, potentially proclaiming clean margins on invaded tissue.

Intended Use & Target Population.
SpotGuard targets translational pathologists and computational oncologists analyzing solid tumor biopsies, operating as an interactive conformal gatekeeper deployed alongside deconvolution pipelines to prune uncalibrated spot calls.


================================================================================
2. METHOD
================================================================================

Compositional Nonconformity on the Simplex.
Cell proportions live on the closed unit simplex Delta^{K-1} = { v in R_+^K : sum_{k=1}^K v_k = 1 } with proportions summing to one. SpotGuard computes base predictions through the maximum a posteriori solution for Dirichlet-multinomial distributions (Kleshchevnikov et al., 2022). In practice, we find that boundary log singularities make sparse proportions ill-defined, so we define nonconformity as the Total Variation (TV) distance:
S_TV(x, y) = 1/2 ||y - y_hat||_1 = 1/2 sum_{k=1}^K |y_k - y_hat_k| in [0, 1],
which is well-defined everywhere on the closed simplex.

Pseudo-Spot Calibration Procedure.
Since real tissue spots do not have available physical single-cell ground truth, SpotGuard generates M = 1,000 calibration pseudo-spots D_cal = {(x_i^syn, y_i^syn)}_{i=1}^M from reference single cells by sampling over Dirichlet cell-type mixtures y_i^syn ~ Dirichlet(beta), aggregating Poisson transcript counts N_i ~ Poisson(lambda), adding ambient contamination rho ~ Beta(a, b), and matching observed sequencing library depth.

Weighted Conformal Prediction under Covariate Shift.
To adjust for covariate shift between atlas and query data (P_ref(X) != P_query(X)), SpotGuard learns a regularized discriminator d_phi(x) on PCA spot embeddings to predict density-ratio weights:
w(x) = (d_phi(x) / (1 - d_phi(x))) * (M_ref / M_query)
(Tibshirani et al., 2019; Barber et al., 2023). We require a 5-fold cross-validated Realism Gate (0.60 <= AUC <= 0.80) and clip weights at the 95th percentile to safeguard the Effective Sample Size (ESS = 314.5). Each query spot receives a weighted quantile defining a valid simplex prediction ball (Angelopoulos & Bates, 2021):
C_alpha(x_{n+1}) = { y in Delta^{K-1} : S_TV(x_{n+1}, y) <= q_hat_alpha(x_{n+1}) }.
Under joint distribution shift, worst-case coverage error is strictly bounded by the total variation distance between reference and query distributions:
|P_{(X,Y)~Q}(y in C_alpha(X)) - (1 - alpha)| <= d_TV(P_{X,Y}, Q_{X,Y})
(Barber et al., 2023).

Selective Gatekeeping Mechanism.
SpotGuard's selective gatekeeping mechanism flags and masks a spot if and only if its prediction set covers a critical treatment threshold (e.g., CD8+ >= 5%) or surpasses the safe uncertainty radius tau_safe.

System Architecture & Deployment.
We implement SpotGuard using a Python calibration backend and interactive Canvas frontend on GitHub Pages providing sub-16ms (>60 fps) recomputation during live alpha tuning.

Shift Performance & Gatekeeping Accuracy.
Under cross-platform shift, density-ratio weighting prevents coverage collapse (89.4% vs. 84.6% unweighted, AURC = 0.138). In mild cross-donor shift, SpotGuard retains nominal coverage (89.8%) compared to unweighted conformal prediction without adding unnecessary bulk, confirming that SpotGuard exhibits no-harm behavior even when donors are shifted minimally. Critically, we show that the selective gatekeeper allows us to mask 18.4% of spots with 91.3% precision and 88.5% recall against spots deemed out-of-coverage, resulting in a 66.2% error reduction in mean compositional Total Variation error on retained tissue (from 0.142 to 0.048). The Bayesian Joint Confidence Interval reduces to 1.0%–2.0%, as an unadjusted Cartesian product of marginals makes the Bayesian Joint Confidence Interval a hypercube, failing to account for the simplex sum constraint and ignoring compositional covariances, which leads to geometric over-exclusion of the true fractions.


================================================================================
4. DISCUSSION, LIMITATIONS, & ACCESS
================================================================================

Clinical Utility & Access.
A single scalar uncertainty radius can provide clinically useful gatekeeping information for pathologists on intractable high-dimensional posterior distributions. Intractable covariates motivate tools that reduce them to scalars.
- Web App: https://the-mathking.github.io/ML4HDemo/
- Video Demo: https://youtu.be/u3NUaWljdgo
- Code Repository: https://github.com/The-MathKing/ML4HDemo

Limitations & Scope.
SpotGuard uses reference single-cell pools to define this uncertainty region, which may not contain all possible rare tumor states. While density-ratio weighting addresses covariate shift in spot gene expression (P(X)), extreme biological label shifts (P(Y|X)) require ongoing validation against physical multiplex immunofluorescence co-stains.
