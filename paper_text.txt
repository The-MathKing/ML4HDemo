================================================================================
ABSTRACT
================================================================================
Spatial transcriptomics (ST) deconvolution models (e.g., cell2location, RCTD) infer cell-type proportions across tissue spots, but unadjusted Bayesian posterior intervals suffer severe miscalibration under atlas-to-query distribution shifts. We present SpotGuard, an operational, interactive conformal gatekeeping system for ST deconvolution. SpotGuard formulates nonconformity via Total Variation (TV) geometry on the simplex Delta^{K-1} and adapts to atlas shift via density-ratio weighted conformal prediction. Integrated into a sub-16ms interactive workstation with live miscoverage (alpha) slider controls, SpotGuard dynamically prunes uncalibrated spots—recovering 89.4% coverage under severe cross-platform shift (vs. 84.6% unweighted, while Bayesian joint intervals collapse to 1.4%) and reducing compositional TV error on retained tissue by 66.2% (AURC = 0.138).

Keywords: Spatial transcriptomics, deconvolution, conformal prediction, distribution shift, oncology.


================================================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

Clinical Problem & Importance.
Spatial transcriptomics (ST) profiles gene expression across intact biopsies (Marx, 2021; Rao et al., 2021). Because sequencing captures multi-cellular mixtures (55 um spots, 5–30 cells), computational deconvolution models—such as cell2location (Kleshchevnikov et al., 2022) and RCTD (Cable et al., 2022)—infer cell-type proportion vectors y_hat in Delta^{K-1}. In oncology, these maps guide vital decisions: quantifying cytotoxic CD8+ T-cell infiltration, detecting tertiary lymphoid structures, and defining surgical margins.

Failure Mode of Current Approaches.
Deconvolution models rely on reference scRNA-seq atlases. When atlases and patient tissues diverge due to platform (10x Chromium vs. Slide-seq), preservation (FFPE vs. fresh frozen), or demographics, exchangeability fails. Bayesian posteriors become overconfident: clinicians cannot distinguish correct from erroneous spot calls, risking declaring clear margins on invaded tissue.

Intended Use & Target Population.
SpotGuard is designed for translational pathologists and computational oncologists analyzing solid tumor biopsies, operating as an interactive conformal gatekeeper deployed alongside deconvolution pipelines to prune uncalibrated spot calls.


================================================================================
2. METHOD
================================================================================

Compositional Nonconformity on the Simplex.
Cell proportions inhabit the closed simplex Delta^{K-1} = { v in R_+^K : sum_{k=1}^K v_k = 1 }. Base predictions y_hat are obtained via Dirichlet-multinomial MAP estimation (Kleshchevnikov et al., 2022). Because sparse proportions yield boundary log singularities, SpotGuard defines nonconformity via Total Variation (TV) distance:
S_TV(x, y) = 1/2 ||y - y_hat||_1 = 1/2 sum_{k=1}^K |y_k - y_hat_k| in [0, 1],
defined everywhere on the closed simplex.

Pseudo-Spot Calibration Procedure.
Because physical single-cell truth is unobservable in tissue spots, SpotGuard synthesizes M = 1,000 calibration pseudo-spots D_cal = {(x_i^syn, y_i^syn)}_{i=1}^M from reference single cells by sampling mixtures y_i^syn ~ Dirichlet(beta), aggregating counts N_i ~ Poisson(lambda), injecting ambient contamination rho ~ Beta(a, b), and matching empirical library depth.

Weighted Conformal Prediction under Covariate Shift.
To correct for atlas-to-query distribution shifts (P_ref(X) != P_query(X)), SpotGuard trains a regularized discriminator d_phi(x) on PCA spot embeddings to compute density-ratio weights:
w(x) = (d_phi(x) / (1 - d_phi(x))) * (M_ref / M_query)
(Tibshirani et al., 2019; Barber et al., 2023). We enforce a 5-fold cross-validated Realism Gate (0.60 <= AUC <= 0.80) and clip weights at the 95th percentile to protect Effective Sample Size (ESS = 314.5). For query spot x_{n+1}, the weighted (1 - alpha) quantile q_hat_alpha(x_{n+1}) defines an approximately valid simplex region (Angelopoulos & Bates, 2021):
C_alpha(x_{n+1}) = { y in Delta^{K-1} : S_TV(x_{n+1}, y) <= q_hat_alpha(x_{n+1}) }.
Under joint distribution shift, the worst-case coverage gap is bounded by the total variation distance:
|P_{(X,Y)~Q}(y in C_alpha(X)) - (1 - alpha)| <= d_TV(P_{X,Y}, Q_{X,Y})
(Barber et al., 2023).

Selective Gatekeeping Mechanism.
SpotGuard converts set predictions into binary clinical decisions: spot x is flagged and masked iff its prediction set C_alpha(x) straddles a critical therapeutic threshold (e.g., CD8+ >= 5%, or q_hat_alpha(x) > tau_safe).

System Architecture & Deployment.
SpotGuard pairs a Python calibration backend with an interactive Canvas frontend on GitHub Pages, achieving sub-16ms (>60 fps) recomputation during live alpha manipulation.

Shift Performance & Gatekeeping Accuracy.
Under cross-platform shift, density-ratio weighting prevents a 4.8% coverage collapse (89.4% vs. 84.6% unweighted), achieving an Area Under the Risk-Coverage curve (AURC) of 0.138. Under mild cross-donor shift, SpotGuard preserves nominal coverage (89.8%) matching unweighted CP without inflating volume, confirming no-harm behavior under minimal shift.
Crucially, evaluating the selective gatekeeper shows SpotGuard masks 18.4% of spots with 91.3% precision and 88.5% recall against out-of-coverage spots, reducing mean compositional TV error from 0.142 to 0.048 on retained tissue (a 66.2% error reduction).
The Bayesian Joint CI (1.0%–2.0%) fails because taking an unadjusted Cartesian product of marginals creates a hypercube that ignores the simplex sum constraint (sum y_k = 1) and compositional covariances, geometrically over-excluding true fractions.


================================================================================
4. DISCUSSION, LIMITATIONS, & ACCESS
================================================================================

Clinical Utility & Access.
Scalar TV radius q_hat_alpha provides actionable gatekeeping for pathologists over intractable high-dimensional posterior tensors.
- Web App: https://the-mathking.github.io/ML4HDemo/
- Demo Video: https://youtu.be/u3NUaWljdgo
- Source Code: https://github.com/The-MathKing/ML4HDemo

Limitations & Scope.
SpotGuard's calibration relies on reference single-cell pools, which may omit rare tumor states. Furthermore, density-ratio weighting addresses covariate shift (P(X)); extreme biological label shifts (P(Y|X)) require ongoing validation against physical multiplex immunofluorescence co-stains.
